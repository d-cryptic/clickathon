== 8 · What is deliberately NOT wired ==

  v_ev_model_input exists; sql/30_build_intervals.sql and sql/90_reconcile.sql still read
  ev_raw directly. Switching them is a TWO-FILE change (model and gate must move together
  or the gate correctly fails on any day where the quarantine is non-empty) and both files
  are owned by other lanes. Proposal + decision table: docs/adr/0025. On the provided file
  the point is moot — zero rows quarantine, the view IS ev_raw (section 4).
