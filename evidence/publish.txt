PHASE 7 — DIMENSION CHANGE. Late events flip a published interval's platform.

  Dimension attribution is the DOMINANT value among an interval's events
  (ADR 0008/0009). A burst of late events under a new platform outvotes the
  old one, so the re-derived interval moves to a different dimension tuple:
  every minute it covers must be RETRACTED from the old platform's user
  buckets and hour curves, and credited to the new one's.
