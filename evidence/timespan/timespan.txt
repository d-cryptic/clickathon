WHAT IS BEING SCALED
  The CALENDAR, not the audience — the complement of evidence/scale.txt.
  Sessions keep the provided file's shape (length, beats, bursts, pauses,
  gaps, dimensions — all fitted by tools/scale-gen.sql) but their START
  TIMES follow a synthetic multi-day profile:
    * diurnal curve: overnight floor, midday bump (13:00), prime-time peak
      centred 21:00 — not the provided file's single launch-event spike
    * weekday/weekend rhythm: weekends x1.35
    * seasonal drift: +/-12% sinusoid over the year
    * EVENT DAYS every 45 days (d%45==7): x2.2 traffic with a sharp 20:30
      spike — the big-match shape that stresses partition heterogeneity
    * hot-content rotation each 30-day epoch: 65% of picks come from a
      rotated Zipf head (titles launch and decay), 35% evergreen
    * sessions cross midnight naturally (counted per point below)
  Volume is held CONSTANT across spans 12/60/180 so any change is caused
  by span alone; a constant-span density pair isolates the other axis.

  DESIGNED TRUTH: every point carries probe blocks on reserved platform
  TIMESPAN_PROBE (40 sessions 20:00-20:29 + 30 sessions 23:45-00:14, on
  day 0, a month-end mid-span day, and the last day) whose minute-by-
  minute concurrency is known in closed form — a THIRD implementation of
  the counting spec, python sets vs arraySplit vs window functions, same
  as tools/unseen-gen.sh. The rest of the stream has no analytic answer;
  there the reconcile gate (delta tier vs interval expansion) is the
  check, run at every point.
