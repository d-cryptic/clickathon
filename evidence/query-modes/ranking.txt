READING THIS TABLE
  * The floor is one MergeTree granule (8,192 rows) times the columns touched.
    Everything at 8,192-8,193 rows sits AT that floor — nothing left to optimise.
  * POINT MODE IS NOT CHEAPER THAN INTERVAL MODE. At hour and day grain a point IS
    one stored row and costs the granule floor; at minute grain a point costs the
    same partial-hour scan an interval does, because both read the same delta rows.
    pm01/im05/im01 are byte-identical at 597,993 — one minute and 74 minutes cost the same.
  * pm01 (597,993 B) vs pm02 (319,464 B): the v_cc_window_range form always probes the
    hour tier even when the range holds no whole hours, so a bare point lookup pays
    1.87x for provenance columns it does not need. Use pm02 to ASK, pm01 to AUDIT.
  * RAGGED vs ALIGNED is the largest structural gap in this table: im01 (ragged,
    597,993 B) vs im02 (aligned, 278,529 B) = 2.15x, over the SAME two hours. And
    id01 spans 13 days for 278,529 B — identical to the 2-hour aligned range, because
    an hour-aligned range is O(1 granule) in length. Ragged edges, not range length,
    are what a minute-tier read costs.
  * tr03 is the most expensive shape here precisely because it computes the wrong
    answer AND the right one AND the independent truth side by side. It is a demo.
